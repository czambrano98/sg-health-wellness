Consistency Over Perfection

Let’s thrive without burning out

Hello! My name is Carine, and I’m running for 1Y Health & Wellness Chair.

My health and mindfulness journey began after I graduated from college: instead of living my beautifully curated postgraduate plans, I was navigating a global pandemic. During this time, my anxiety reached unbearable levels, which pushed me to refocus and prioritize my mental health. My path to resetting and rebalancing led me to mindfulness and yoga, two practices that remain at the core of my self-care. Through them, I’ve learned to focus on consistency rather than perfection, which I believe is a mindset that can help all of us navigate our first year at SOM.

My four priorities

  1. Providing accessible and inclusive fitness programming so everyone at every level of practice feels welcome to join (and have fun!). Before SOM, I managed clients from Des Moines to Chile. I’m well-skilled in planning events that make everyone feel welcomed and engaged.
  2. Enhancing the Fit for Thought experience by tapping into existing partnerships and resources. My six years in marketing taught me how to build partnerships, advocate for new ideas, and convince people that they were their idea all along.
  3. Offering regular, mindfulness-focused events that build community and create space to recharge outside of academics and recruiting. As a Founding ERG Chapter Lead at my former company, I organized quarterly events that brought together over 20 people in an informal, community-focused setting.
  4. Listening to student needs and ensuring our wellness priorities reflect them. Outside of work, I volunteered as a high school mentor, which gave me plenty of practice listening before jumping in with solutions.

I’m so excited about the opportunity to serve as your 1Y Health & Wellness Chair, and I’m looking forward to helping our class grow, connect, and thrive during our first year at SOM!

Pre-SOM experience

Before SOM, I was a Sr. Associate at Nasdaq, where I worked for four years. In this role, I advised clients across the U.S. and Latin America, translating their objectives into marketing and distribution activations. Throughout my career, I've focused on building a cross-disciplinary skill set by working across functions, industries, and the private and public sectors.
